Why Scene Lens exists

Every day a flood of information rushes past us: people, vehicles, objects, moods. Most of it goes unnoticed. Scene Lens makes it visible, so you can see how many things are around you and what that says about the place you are in.

01

Detect

Google's MediaPipe Object Detector (EfficientDet-Lite2) finds 80 everyday object classes and draws a box around each one, many times per second.

02

Count

A small tracker follows boxes from frame to frame, so a moving car is counted once. You get a running total and the count of the current frame.

03

Go deeper

Optionally, a face landmark model reads facial expressions of detected people and sorts them into moods. Faces that are not visible stay plain "people". No identification, nothing is stored.

04

Understand

The mix of objects suggests a scene, such as an office, a street or a highway, and is compared with rough typical counts for that place.

Everything runs locally in your browser with WebAssembly and WebGPU. No upload, no server, no account. Built for desktop Chrome.
